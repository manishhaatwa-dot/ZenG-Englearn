Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your ZenG-Englearn account and associated personal data.

To delete your account, use the account deletion option available inside the ZenG-Englearn application.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you confirm account deletion, your ZenG-Englearn account and associated personal data stored for your account will be permanently deleted.

  • Account and authentication information will be deleted.
  • Profile information will be deleted.
  • Learning-related account data will be deleted.
  • Personal data associated with your account will be deleted.

Deleted account data cannot be recovered after the deletion process is completed.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Please make sure you want to delete your account before confirming the deletion.
