Privacy Policy

ZenG-Englearn respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the ZenG-Englearn application and website.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ZenG-Englearn may collect:

  • Account information: Your email address and authentication information used to create and access your account.
  • Profile information: Your display name and profile photo if you choose to provide them.
  • Learning information: Information related to your learning activity and use of the application's features.
  • Communication information: Information you provide when using communication or chat features.

How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide login, email verification and password recovery.
  • Provide learning and educational features.
  • Display your profile information inside the application.
  • Provide communication and chat features where available.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Provide notifications where required for app functionality.

Firebase Services

ZenG-Englearn uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, database, storage and other functionality required for the application to operate.

Information processed through these services is handled according to Google's applicable policies and security practices.

Data Sharing

ZenG-Englearn does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers, such as Firebase, when necessary to provide the application's features, security and technical infrastructure.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your ZenG-Englearn account and associated personal information through the account deletion feature available in the application, where provided.

Children's Privacy

ZenG-Englearn is an English-learning application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through the contact information provided by ZenG-Englearn.
